# Doodle & Dare: Play Store listing (en-US)

**App name (30 max):** Doodle & Dare

**Short description (80 max):**
Draw on your phone, guess on the TV! A party drawing game for 2 to 8 players.

**Full description (4000 max):**
Doodle & Dare turns your living room into a drawing party. One player gets a secret word and draws it on their phone. Everyone else races to guess it, and the drawing appears live on the big screen.

HOW IT WORKS
• Open the game on your TV (Chromecast or Google TV) and scan the QR code. Each phone becomes a controller.
• The drawer picks one of three words and sketches it on their phone.
• Everyone else types guesses. The faster you guess, the more points you score.
• Take turns until the last round, then celebrate on the podium.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• 2 to 8 players, or play in teams.
• 7 languages: English, Portuguese (Portugal and Brazil), Spanish, French, German and Chinese. Each phone uses its own language.
• Hundreds of words in six themes and three difficulty levels.
• Make your own categories and word sets. Great for classrooms, language lessons and family nights.
• A mode for little kids, Pass the Sketch mode, and a Golden Pencil bonus.
• Save your favourite drawings.

GOOD TO KNOW
• You need a TV with Chromecast or a Google TV.
• Guests never pay. The host gets 3 free rooms, then can unlock unlimited rooms with one in-app purchase.
• No account, no ads and no tracking.
